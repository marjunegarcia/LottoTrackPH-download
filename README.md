# LottoTrack PH — Download & Install (Android)

LottoTrack PH shows the latest PCSO lotto results (6/42 to 6/58, 4D, 6D, Swertres, 2D) and helps you track your own numbers:

- 🔔 **Notifications** when new results are out — and when your numbers hit or a ticket wins
- ⏰ **Next draw countdown** and today's draw line-up
- 🎟️ **Ticket checker** — save a ticket for a specific draw and see if it won and the prize tier
- ⭐ **My Numbers** — save your combos and see how many you hit after every draw
- 🔥 **Hot & cold, last seen, and number search** across past draws
- ✨ **Lucky Pick** — random, hot-weighted, or **Dream/Omen** (type what you dreamed: ahas, isda, kasal…)
- 📱 **Home screen widget** with today's Swertres & 2D
- 📤 **Share results as an image** to Facebook or Messenger

## 📲 How to install (sideload)

This app isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android will ask you to confirm a couple of things the first time. That's normal.

1. **Download the APK**
   Open the **Download LottoTrackPH.apk** link above on your phone.
   If your browser warns *"This type of file can harm your device"*, tap **Download anyway**.

2. **Open the downloaded file**
   Tap the download notification, or open your **Files / My Files** app → **Downloads** → tap the APK.

3. **Allow installs from your browser (first time only)**
   Android will say *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*
   Tap **Settings** → turn on **Allow from this source** → press **Back**.

4. **Tap Install**
   If **Google Play Protect** says it doesn't recognize the app, tap **More details** → **Install anyway**.
   (It warns because the app isn't from the Play Store, not because something is wrong with it.)

5. **Open the app** and tap **Fetch** to load today's results. 🎉

### Phone-specific tips

| Phone | What you might see |
|---|---|
| **Samsung** | The setting is under **Settings → Apps → ⋮ → Special access → Install unknown apps → (your browser / My Files)** |
| **Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO** | A security scan may run first. Wait for it, then tap **Install anyway** / **Continue installation**. |
| **OPPO / realme / Vivo** | It may ask you to wait a few seconds or confirm with your phone/account password, then tap **Install** / **Install anyway**. |
| **Huawei / Honor** | If asked, allow **Install apps from external sources** for your file manager or browser. |

Tip: after installing, you can turn **Allow from this source** back off for extra safety.

## 🔄 Updating to a new version

**From v1.6.5 on, the app updates itself:** when a new version is out, an **Update available** card appears at the top of Home. Tap **Update now** → it downloads → tap **Update** on the Android screen. (The first time, Android asks you to turn on **Allow from this source** for LottoTrack PH — turn it on, go back, and tap **Install update**.) You can also tap **Check for updates** at the bottom of Home.

Or do it by hand:

1. Download again from the same **Download LottoTrackPH.apk** link.
2. Open it and tap **Update**.

Your saved numbers and draw history are **kept** — don't uninstall first, or you'll lose them.

## ❓ Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **"App not installed"** or **"package conflicts with an existing package"** | You have a very early test version (before v1.1.1). Uninstall the old LottoTrack PH once, then install the new APK. Future updates install normally. |
| **"There was a problem parsing the package"** | The download didn't finish. Delete the file and download again. |
| **Install button is greyed out** | Close any screen-overlay or bubble apps (chat heads, screen dimmers) and try again. |
| **Fetch says it couldn't reach the results sites** | Check your internet connection and try again. Results usually appear 15–60 minutes after each draw. |
